# Literature and benchmark starting points

The references below support the choice of model families. They do not validate the placeholder parameters used in this project.

1. **Kinetic Monte Carlo simulation for semiconductor processing: A review.** *Progress in Materials Science* 92 (2018), 1–32. DOI: [10.1016/j.pmatsci.2017.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmatsci.2017.09.003). Covers implantation, annealing, defects, dopant activation/deactivation and epitaxial growth, including object and lattice KMC.
2. **Battaile, C. C.** The kinetic Monte Carlo method: foundation, implementation, and application. *Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering* 197 (2008), 3386–3398. DOI: [10.1016/j.cma.2008.03.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2008.03.010). KMC foundations and applications including vacancy diffusion, grain growth and film deposition.
3. **Kinetic Monte Carlo Simulation of Chemical Vapor Deposition.** *Annual Review of Materials Research* 32 (2002), 297. DOI: [10.1146/annurev.matsci.32.012102.110247](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.matsci.32.012102.110247). Surface adsorption, desorption, diffusion and incorporation in CVD.
4. **Atomic scale models of ion implantation and dopant diffusion in silicon.** *Thin Solid Films* / related semiconductor processing literature. [ScienceDirect record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040609000007574). Describes BCA cascade input and KMC defect/dopant evolution; use the paper's full bibliographic metadata when citing it in a formal report.
5. **Computer simulation of ion implantation into crystalline targets.** *Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research B* 64 (1992), 706–710. DOI: [10.1016/0168-583X(92)95562-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0168-583X(92)95562-6). Binary-collision implantation simulation in crystalline targets.
6. **SRIM / TRIM.** [SRIM official website](https://www.srim.org/). Benchmark tool for ion stopping, range and damage estimates. Record the SRIM version and all target/beam settings in comparisons.
7. **Ruan, S. and Schuh, C. A.** Kinetic Monte Carlo simulations of nanocrystalline film deposition. *Journal of Applied Physics* 107 (2010), 073512. DOI: [10.1063/1.3331986](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3331986). KMC model of film deposition and microstructure formation.

When using any reference quantitatively, inspect the original paper and copy the exact material, temperature, crystal orientation, defect charge state, parameter definitions and uncertainty. Do not transfer a barrier from one defect or dopant configuration to another without justification.
